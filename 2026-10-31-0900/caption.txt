Your INSERT failed with "duplicate key value violates unique constraint". One clause fixes it.

INSERT ... ON CONFLICT is how you write a PostgreSQL upsert. DO NOTHING skips the row that already exists. DO UPDATE changes it instead.

Inside DO UPDATE, EXCLUDED is the row you tried to insert. In our test, orders = c.orders + EXCLUDED.orders took Ravi from 3 orders to 4.

Two things will bite you. DO UPDATE needs a conflict target backed by a unique index or constraint. And if one batch has the same key twice, you get "ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE command cannot affect row a second time".

Every statement ran on PostgreSQL 16, and the outputs on the slides are the real ones.
