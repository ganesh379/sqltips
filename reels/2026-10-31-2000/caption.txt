What happens inside PostgreSQL after you press Enter on a SELECT?

Your query reaches port 5432, gets its own backend process, then goes through the parser, rewriter, planner and executor.

The executor looks for the 8 KB page in shared_buffers first. If it is not there, PostgreSQL asks the operating system, which has its own cache too, so the read may never touch the disk.
