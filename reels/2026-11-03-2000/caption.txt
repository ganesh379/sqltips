You ran TRUNCATE, then ROLLBACK. Is the data back in PostgreSQL?

Yes, if the TRUNCATE ran inside BEGIN. The PostgreSQL docs say TRUNCATE is transaction-safe: the truncation is rolled back if the surrounding transaction does not commit. Our orders table went from 3 rows to 0 and back to 3.

The trap for a PostgreSQL DBA: without BEGIN, psql autocommits the TRUNCATE right away. A ROLLBACK after that only prints "WARNING:  there is no transaction in progress" and the rows stay gone.

Tested on PostgreSQL 16 in psql.
