What does a PostgreSQL DBA really do every day?

A PostgreSQL DBA keeps the database available, secure, fast and recoverable, and ready to grow: monitoring and replication, roles and privileges, slow queries and indexes, backups that are actually tested with restores, and capacity before it becomes an outage.

PostgreSQL also needs routine maintenance. VACUUM reclaims space from updated and deleted rows and refreshes planner statistics. And the PostgreSQL project recommends always running the current minor release, because minor releases carry bug and security fixes.
